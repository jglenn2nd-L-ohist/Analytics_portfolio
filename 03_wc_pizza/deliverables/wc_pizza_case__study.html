05
What To Do About It
Recommendations
1
Bring Staffing to Budgeted Levels
Downtown and Smyrna are running below scheduled headcount on their highest-volume days. Every understaffed game day is a throughput constraint on the most profitable days of the year.
Priority: Downtown · Smyrna · Before July 15
2
Fix Scheduling Logic — Staff to Volume
Overtime is occurring on slow days across multiple locations. The schedule is flat when it should be dynamic. Match staffing to forecasted order volume to eliminate unnecessary labor cost.
Owner: Store Ops · Timeline: Immediate
3
Prioritize Downtown Inventory for July 15
Classic Pepperoni and chicken pizzas dominate game day sales. Downtown carries the highest volume and margin. Inventory allocation for the final must be weighted toward Downtown to avoid stockouts on the highest-revenue day.
Owner: Merchandising · Timeline: Before July 15

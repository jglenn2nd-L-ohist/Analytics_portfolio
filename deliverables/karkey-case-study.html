WHERE d.duration_minutes BETWEEN 1 AND 1439

Finding 01 — Completion Baseline

The 97-Minute Standard

Company-wide average completion time across all 2,000 jobs settled at 97 minutes. This becomes the benchmark for every efficiency comparison in the analysis.

97 min company baseline
Finding 02 — Parts & Time

The 7-Piece Bottleneck

Jobs requiring 7 parts run 5–10% over the 97-minute average — the only parts tier that consistently exceeds baseline. High-part-count jobs are dragging throughput without proportional revenue return.

+5–10% over avg at 7 pieces
Finding 03 — Technician Performance

Rivera Outperforms by 38%

At 2-piece jobs, Rivera generates $4.15/min against a $3.00 company average — a 38% premium. At 5 pieces, Rivera still earns $3.30/min, 10% above average. The gap points to a method worth documenting.

$4.15/min Rivera at 2-piece jobs
Finding 04 — Service Mix

Re-key Runs 6.6% Slower

Ignition Repair completes 6.6% faster than Re-key at comparable revenue levels. The service mix is currently weighted toward the slower service type without a revenue justification.

6.6% Re-key slower than Ignition
04 — Recommendations

Three Moves
for the Director

Every recommendation ties directly back to a data finding — no guesswork, no filler.

1

Scale Rivera's Playbook Across the Team

Rivera earns $4.15/min — 38% above the $3.00 company average. Documenting and training the team on Rivera's methods would close that gap. A 15% lift across all techs translates to tens of thousands in added quarterly revenue.

High Impact
2

Eliminate the 7-Piece Bottleneck

7-part jobs consistently run 5–10% over the 97-minute average, dragging down daily throughput. Pre-staged parts kits or a dedicated workflow for high-part-count jobs could recover significant billable time per technician per week.

Efficiency
3

Rebalance the Service Mix Toward Ignition Repair

Ignition Repair completes 6.6% faster than Re-key at comparable revenue. Shifting marketing and scheduling priority toward Ignition Repair increases daily job capacity without adding headcount.

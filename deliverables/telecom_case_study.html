05 — Key Findings

Six Things the Data Said

Every finding traced directly to the numbers — no assumptions.

Finding 01 — Competition
45%
Competitors Are Winning
Nearly half of all churned customers left specifically for a competitor. Better devices and better offers were cited as the top reasons.
Finding 02 — Contracts
89%
M2M Contracts Are a Liability
Month-to-month customers churn at 89% vs 9% for one-year and 3% for two-year. The contract type is the single strongest structural predictor.
Finding 03 — Loyalty
93%
Referrals Lock In Retention
Customers who made 4+ referrals stayed at a 93% rate. Word-of-mouth is not just a growth tool — it is a retention signal.
Finding 04 — Dependents
93%
More Dependents, More Loyalty
Customers with dependents stayed at 92–93%. Family plans create switching friction that keeps customers anchored to the network.
Finding 05 — Demographics
2×
Single Customers Churn Twice as Often
Single customers churn at 18% vs 10% for married. No-dependent, no-referral single customers on M2M contracts are the highest-risk segment.
Finding 06 — Attitude
21%
Support Experience Drives Departure
15% cited attitude of support staff and 21% cited competitor made a better offer — both are controllable factors with clear action paths.

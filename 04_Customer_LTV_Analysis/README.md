# 04 Customer LTV Analysis

## Business Context
Based in the UK with an international footprint that spans across greater than 4000 distinct customers. It is imperative to know the customer based and their patterns to best allocate resources and maximize revenue. The goal of this analysis is to reveal customer habits and patterns across borders.
## Business Questions
   **Q1** What is the profile of our customers?
   **Q2** Buying habits: frequency/recency/order value
   **Q3** Do patterns vary by country?
   **Q4** How do customer cohorts retain and grow over time?
   **Q5** What is the lifetime value by cohort?
